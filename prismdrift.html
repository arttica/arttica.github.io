1. Game data and device permissions

The game stores your current puzzle, block positions, move history, completed levels, star results, tutorial state and preferences locally. It also stores your remaining daily and earned hints and the date used to refresh the daily allowance.

These records support resuming a puzzle, unlocking levels, undoing moves, showing results and remembering settings. The game does not require a player account or send your saved puzzle progress or hint balance to an Arttica game server. Your device's backup service may include this information, depending on your Apple settings.

The current game does not request access to your precise GPS location, contacts, camera, photo library or microphone for recording. Approximate location can still be inferred by advertising services from your IP address. No separate general-purpose analytics or third-party mediation SDK is integrated into the current app; advertising services can nevertheless collect their own measurement and diagnostic data.

2. Third-party advertising

Optional rewarded videos are supplied through Google AdMob, using the Google Mobile Ads SDK. The game requests non-personalized, contextual ads, rather than ads selected from your past activity across unrelated apps or websites.

Non-personalized advertising still involves data processing. Google and the advertising participants involved in an ad may process:

  • Network and location information: IP address and general location inferred from it.
  • Device and app information: device or app identifiers, operating-system information and other technical details needed to deliver ads. Advertising identifiers are subject to iOS restrictions and permissions.
  • Advertising activity: ad requests, impressions, video views, clicks and other interactions.
  • Diagnostics and performance: crash information and measurements relating to launches, responsiveness or resource use.

Advertising services use this information to deliver and measure ads, limit repeated impressions, detect fraud and invalid traffic, and maintain or improve their services. Cookies, local storage or similar identifiers may be used for those purposes, including within ad content. Non-personalized ads are not necessarily identifier-free or consent-free.

The current app does not request Apple's App Tracking Transparency authorization. This does not mean that advertising services collect no information. Advertisers or other participants may vary by ad and region; applicable consent messages provide vendor information and choices. See the provider links in Section 13.

3. When ad processing occurs

On a launch without a verified no-ads upgrade, the app can contact Google's consent service and prepare or preload a rewarded ad. Advertising-related network activity can therefore occur before you choose to watch a video. Choosing not to watch a video avoids playback but does not, by itself, disable ad preparation.

The app checks your purchase entitlement before preparing ads and uses Google's consent status to determine whether ad requests are permitted. Consent-status requests may be needed before that determination can be made.

After Apple verifies that you own Remove Ads + Unlimited Hints, the app stops making new ad requests and provides unlimited hints. On subsequent launches with verified ownership, it does not start the advertising or advertising-consent flow. This does not erase information providers received before the upgrade or stop Apple purchase verification.

The daily free hint allowance does not require watching an ad. An optional completed rewarded video grants three additional hints; the completion callback updates your hint balance locally.

4. Consent and advertising choices

We use Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to obtain and manage applicable advertising privacy choices. Where required, a privacy message explains relevant purposes and providers and allows you to accept, reject or adjust choices. UMP may process technical information and store consent decisions or consent-related identifiers to determine and remember the applicable choices.

When Google requires an ongoing privacy-options entry point, open Settings → Ad Privacy Choices in the game to review or change your selections. The availability and contents of that form depend on your region and Google's applicable requirements. If the entry is not shown, you can contact arttica.is@gmail.com with a privacy request.

You may withdraw consent where processing relies on consent. Withdrawal applies going forward and does not make earlier lawful processing invalid. The effect on advertising depends on the applicable choices; limited ads may be available, or video hints may be unavailable. Declining optional advertising consent does not prevent basic gameplay or the daily free hints.

Your device's privacy settings and Google's own controls may offer additional choices. Those controls apply to the services they cover and do not necessarily remove all data processing described here. You do not have to buy an upgrade to submit a privacy-rights request.

5. Apple and in-app purchases

Apple processes the optional one-time upgrade through the App Store. The game uses StoreKit purchase information, including the product purchased, verified ownership and transaction status, to unlock the upgrade, restore purchases and respond to refunds or revocations.

Arttica does not receive your full payment-card details or Apple Account password. Apple handles payment information and its purchase records under its own privacy policy. Apple may also process platform diagnostics or backups according to your device and account settings.

Deleting the game does not cancel or delete the purchase held by Apple. Use Restore Purchases in Settings to restore eligible ownership on the Apple Account used for the purchase. Contact Apple for App Store billing or refund requests.

6. Support communications

If you email arttica.is@gmail.com, we receive your email address, any sender name your email service supplies, the contents of your message and any attachments you choose to send. This can include screenshots or diagnostic details you provide to help us understand an issue.

We use that information to respond, troubleshoot problems and keep relevant support records. Our Gmail email service processes communications to deliver and store them. Emailing support is optional; send only the information needed for your request. We do not use support correspondence for advertising targeting.

Support correspondence is retained only as long as reasonably needed to handle your request, maintain relevant support records, resolve disputes or meet legal obligations. You may request deletion, subject to applicable exceptions.

Advertising, consent and purchase records held by Google, Apple or other providers follow their retention policies and applicable law. Deleting the app, changing consent or purchasing the upgrade does not automatically delete their previously collected records. Provider privacy controls and request channels are listed below.
