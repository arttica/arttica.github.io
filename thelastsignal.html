Effective September 28, 2026 · The Last Signal, version 1.0

Your privacy, at a glance.

The Last Signal is an iPhone and iPad game operated by Arttica Inc. This policy explains how information is handled when you play, buy Signal Cores, contact us, or visit this page.

No account is required to play. Your gameplay progress stays on your device. This version has no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs and does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

Game progress
Saved locally on your device, not sent to an Arttica game server.
Payments
Processed by Apple. The game uses verified purchase records to deliver your Signal Cores.
Support
Information you choose to email us is used to respond to your request.
01 / ON YOUR DEVICE

Your game data

The game stores the information it needs to remember your progress and choices, including:

  • Completed levels, collected fragments, stars, best runs, and gameplay statistics.
  • Daily rewards, revive allowances, and currency balances.
  • Owned skins, equipped cosmetics, and sound, haptic, and visual preferences.
  • Purchase-delivery records used to prevent the same transaction from granting cores twice.

This information is stored in the app’s local files and settings. The game does not transmit it to an Arttica server and does not offer an Arttica account or cloud-save service. Your device backups may include this data according to your Apple settings.

The game does not request access to your contacts, photos, microphone, camera, or precise location.

02 / SIGNAL CORES

Purchases through Apple

Apple processes the app purchase and optional in-app purchases. The game receives verified StoreKit transaction information, including the product and transaction identifiers, to deliver Signal Cores and handle unfinished purchases. Delivery identifiers and your core balance are stored on your device.

Arttica does not receive your payment-card details through the game. Apple handles your Apple Account, payment information, purchase history, and related services under its own privacy policy.

Signal Core packs are consumables. Apple purchase sync does not restore packs that were already delivered. Deleting the game may remove remaining cores and unlocked cosmetics along with local progress.

03 / NO CROSS-APP TRACKING

Advertising, analytics & sharing

This version contains no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs. It does not use an advertising identifier to track you, build an advertising profile, or follow you across other companies’ apps or websites. Arttica does not sell your personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Apple may process information as part of operating the App Store and its platform services, under Apple’s own policies and your device settings. That processing is separate from the local game saves described above.

04 / WHEN YOU CONTACT US

Support information

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to provide, such as a description of an issue, device details, screenshots, or purchase references. We use that information to reply, investigate your issue, and provide support.

Please do not send passwords, payment-card numbers, or unrelated sensitive information. Email and website service providers may process correspondence or connection information to help deliver those services. We may also disclose information where required by law or reasonably necessary to address fraud, security, or legal claims.

05 / KEEPING & REMOVING DATA

Retention & deletion

Local game data: Saves remain on your device until removed. Deleting the app removes its local data; offloading the app may retain it. Backups are managed separately through your Apple settings. Arttica cannot retrieve local saves from your device on your behalf.

Support correspondence: We keep information you send for as long as reasonably needed to respond, resolve the issue, and meet applicable legal or recordkeeping obligations. You can request deletion by contacting us. We may retain information when required by law or needed to resolve an outstanding dispute.

Apple records: Removing the app or asking Arttica to delete support correspondence does not delete Apple’s purchase history or account records. Manage those records directly with Apple.

06 / YOUR CONTROL

Your choices & privacy requests

You choose whether to make optional in-app purchases or contact support. Manage device backups and Apple privacy preferences in your device or Apple Account settings.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal information you have provided, or to make other privacy requests. Email arttica.is@gmail.com to ask a question or make a request. We may need enough information to verify that the request relates to you. Information held only by Apple or another provider must be managed with that provider.

07 / FAMILIES

Children’s privacy

The game does not require players to submit a name, email address, or date of birth. Arttica does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through the game. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, for example in a support email, contact us so we can review and remove it as appropriate.

Parents and guardians can manage purchase restrictions and parental controls using Apple’s device and account settings.

08 / READING THIS PAGE

This privacy website

This page contains no advertising, analytics scripts, contact forms, or embedded social media. It does not itself set cookies or store browser preferences. Its hosting provider may process standard connection information, such as an IP address, requested page, and browser information, to deliver and protect the website. This is separate from data stored by the game.

Following a link to Apple or another website takes you to a service governed by its own privacy policy.

09 / KEEPING YOU INFORMED

Changes to this policy

We will update this policy if the game’s data practices change. The effective date above identifies the latest revision. Where additional notice or consent is required, we will provide it before using information for the new purpose.
